What we collect

When you use Request a Solution, contact us, or otherwise reach out, we may collect your name, phone number, location, and any details or documents you choose to share about the service you need.

How we use it

Information you share is used only to understand and deliver the solution you've requested, and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Documents you share with us

Where a service requires a document (for example, an existing CV or an ID for a government-service request), we handle it only for the purpose of completing that request.

Third-party platforms
