About Tiger Empire

Built in Africa. For Africa.

How a small digital-service operation in Kisii became a technology company working to solve real problems.

The Beginning

A cyber café in Ogembo, Kisii

Tiger Empire started as a small digital-service operation — printing, typing, scanning and basic computer help for the local community in Ogembo, Kisii.

The Problem

Digital services shouldn't be this hard to access

People and businesses increasingly need help with things technology should make simple — government registrations, a professional CV, a basic website, or just knowing which tool to use. Too often, getting that help means navigating confusing systems alone.

The Evolution

From a physical desk to a digital hub

Tiger Empire is expanding beyond the physical cyber café into a broader set of digital solutions — AI-assisted work, business technology, digital skills training and remote service delivery — while keeping the same hands-on, get-it-done approach that started it all.

The Mission

Make useful technology practical and accessible

We don't sell technology for the sake of technology. We use it to solve problems — for a student applying for a course, a business owner setting up online, or someone who just needs a document sorted out properly.

The Vision

A serious African technology brand

We're building Tiger Empire into a technology company capable of delivering digital solutions well beyond Ogembo — to anyone in Kenya who needs practical help getting something done.

Have something to solve?

Let's get to work.

Whether you're in Kisii or anywhere else in Kenya, most of what we do can start remotely.
