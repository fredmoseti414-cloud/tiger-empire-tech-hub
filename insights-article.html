Getting Your Government Documents Sorted Without the Runaround

Most people don't struggle with government services because the services themselves are complicated. They struggle because nobody explains the process clearly, the platforms assume a level of digital confidence many people haven't had reason to build, and one small mistake — a wrong document, a missed step — can send you back to the start.

Where things usually go wrong

A few patterns come up again and again with eCitizen, SHA, NTSA and KRA services:

  • Using the wrong account type, or an account that was never fully verified.
  • Submitting documents that don't match what the system expects — wrong format, wrong size, or missing a required field.
  • Not knowing which office or department a specific issue actually belongs to.
  • Losing track of reference numbers or confirmation details needed later.

What actually helps

The fastest way through any of these systems is knowing exactly what's required before you start — the right documents, in the right format, submitted the right way the first time. That's most of what assistance actually looks like in practice: less about having special access, more about not repeating avoidable mistakes.

It also helps to have someone double-check the details before submission, and to keep a proper record of what was submitted and when — something that's easy to lose track of when you're doing it once, under pressure, on your own.

If you'd rather have it handled

This is exactly what our Online Service Assistance covers — eCitizen, KRA, NTSA, SHA, HELB and related services, done properly the first time. Most of it can start remotely, wherever you are in Kenya.
